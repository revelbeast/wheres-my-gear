About Where’s My Gear

Where’s My Gear is a mobile inventory and organization platform designed to help users catalog, organize, and track personal equipment, tools, travel gear, emergency supplies, outdoor equipment, and household belongings.

The app uses barcode and UPC scanning to help identify products and enrich personal inventory records with product metadata, including product titles, brand information, images, and descriptions when available. This improves organization, searchability, and reduces manual entry for users maintaining personal inventories.

Core Features

  • Barcode and UPC product identification
  • Inventory organization by vehicle, storage area, or location
  • Equipment tracking and categorization
  • Checklists for travel, preparedness, and gear management
  • Cloud synchronization and secure account storage

The platform does not sell products, manage seller accounts, alter marketplace listings, process orders, manage pricing, or support fulfillment operations.
