About Where’s My Gear

Where’s My Gear is a mobile inventory and organization platform designed to help users catalog, organize, and track personal equipment, tools, travel gear, emergency supplies, outdoor equipment, and household belongings.

The app uses barcode and UPC scanning to help identify products and enrich personal inventory records with product metadata, including product titles, brand information, and images. This improves organization, searchability, and reduces manual entry for users maintaining personal inventories.

Core Features

  • Barcode and UPC product identification
  • Inventory organization by vehicle, storage area, or location
  • Equipment tracking and categorization
  • Checklists for travel, preparedness, and gear management
  • Cloud synchronization and secure account storage

Where’s My Gear is intended for personal inventory organization only. The platform does not sell products, manage Amazon Seller Central accounts, alter product listings, process orders, manage pricing, or support fulfillment operations.

Why Users Choose Where’s My Gear

  • Faster inventory setup through barcode scanning
  • Reduced manual data entry
  • Improved product recognition and organization
  • Better visibility into stored equipment and belongings
  • Easier travel, storage, and preparedness planning

Privacy Policy

Where’s My Gear collects and uses information to provide inventory tracking and synchronization features across devices.

Information We Collect

  • Email address and account authentication data
  • Vehicle, compartment, and inventory data
  • Barcode and UPC scan values entered or captured by users
  • Product metadata used for inventory identification, such as product title, brand, image, and description
  • Notes, quantities, and checklist information
  • Photos uploaded by users
  • Subscription and purchase status

How We Use Data

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Sync data across devices
  • Manage subscriptions and premium features
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Provide customer support

Data Storage

Data is stored using Firebase services for authentication, database, and storage, and RevenueCat for subscription management. These providers use industry-standard security measures.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Data is only shared with service providers required to operate the app, provide authentication, cloud storage, subscription management, product identification, barcode lookup, customer support, or when required by law.

When users scan a barcode or UPC, the app may use third-party product information providers to retrieve product metadata for inventory identification. This metadata is used only to help users identify and organize personal belongings.

Data Retention

We retain user inventory records, barcode scan data, product metadata, and account information while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. You may request deletion by contacting support.
